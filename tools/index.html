swisstopo for Garmin

Swisstopo maps on your Garmin watch.

Pre-built map packs of Switzerland for Garmin watches. LK25 raster cartography with a slope-shading and ski/hiking route overlay, sized to fit your watch's tile limit.

Find maps for my tour → How to install

What is this?

A way to put Swiss topographic maps on your Garmin watch. Same LK25 cartography you'd see on a paper map or in SchweizMobil, with a slope-angle and route overlay on top. Not an app. Just files you copy to the watch over USB.

❄ Winter

LK25 Winter base, with a 4-class slope-shading overlay and SAC ski tours coloured by difficulty (blue, red, black).

☀ Summer

LK25 Summer base, with the slope overlay and the Wanderwege network coloured by category (yellow, red, blue).

Switzerland is split into 31 regions

A Garmin watch can hold one regional pack at a time. Pick the region that covers your trips, copy it across, and use it as long as your trips fall inside it. Swap only when you move to a different region.

Winter and summer regions (1 to 20) Summer-only regions (21 to 31) No pre-built pack — use the custom-area picker

Two ways to find the right pack

Not sure which of the 31 regions covers your destination? Pick the workflow that fits.

📍

Drop your GPX (or browse the map)

Drop a tour file (from SchweizMobil, Komoot, Strava, etc.) and we'll tell you which regional pack covers it, usually a single file. No GPX? Open the picker and you can see all 31 regions on a swisstopo basemap and download the one you need.
🎨

Paint a custom area

Going somewhere that crosses two regions, or want a single pack focused on the spots you actually visit? Draw the area you need. We build a custom pack and email it to you, usually within an hour, occasionally longer when the build queue is busy.
🧭

Routable vector map (fēnix 9 Pro, Edge 1050)

Smooth, routable Garmin .img of all of Switzerland or your own area: turn-by-turn navigation and address search on the device. No tile cap. Includes the step-by-step install guide.

Advantages and limitations

Worth reading before you start.

👍 What you get

  • Real swisstopo LK25 cartography, the same look as the paper map.
  • 4-class slope-shading overlay (≥30°, ≥35°, ≥40°, ≥45°) for backcountry awareness.
  • Ski tours coloured by SAC difficulty, hiking trails coloured by category.
  • Works fully offline once copied to the watch.
  • Pick a region by GPX or name, or paint a custom area.

⚠ What to know first

  • The watch can only show one regional pack at a time. If your next outing is in a different region, you swap before you go.
  • Swapping happens by plugging the watch into a computer over USB. There's no in-watch toggle for individual map packs.
  • These are raster maps. They look great but don't give turn-by-turn re-routing the way Garmin's own TopoActive does.
  • Slope shading is advisory only. It does not replace the daily SLF avalanche bulletin or your own judgement on the day.
  • Setup is a manual file copy. The guide is step-by-step, but plan 15 minutes the first time.
Safety: slope shading is derived from the 10 m elevation model and is for awareness only. It does not replace the daily SLF avalanche bulletin, route planning, or on-site judgement. Backcountry travel has inherent risks.

Which Garmin devices does this work on?

Garmin's Custom Maps feature has a tile-count limit baked into the firmware, per device. The published regional packs target the 500-tile tier. If your device has a smaller cap, use the custom-area picker to paint a tighter zone.

✓ Standard regional packs work

Tile cap ≈ 500. Pick any region from the GPX picker and copy the .kmz files across.

  • Epix 2 (Gen 2), Epix Pro 42 / 47 / 51 mm
  • Fenix 8 (43 / 47 / 51 mm AMOLED, Solar MIP)
  • Fenix 7 / 7S / 7X (incl. Pro variants)
  • Fenix 5X (original, 2017)
  • Tactix 7 / 7 Pro, MARQ Gen 2, Quatix 8
  • Forerunner 965
  • GPSMAP 64 / 65 / 66 / 67 family
  • Montana 600 / 650 / 680 / 700 / 700i / 750i
  • Oregon 600 / 650 / 650t
⚠ Custom area required (small tile cap)

Tile cap 100–250. A full region won't fit. Paint a small area around your tours and we email a tile-trimmed pack.

  • Fenix 6 / 6S / 6X (incl. Pro and Solar) — ≈100 tiles
  • Fenix 5 Plus / 5S Plus / 5X Plus — ≈100 tiles
  • Forerunner 945 / 955 — ≈100 tiles
  • Enduro 1 / 2 / 3 — ≈100 tiles
  • Tactix Delta, Quatix 7, MARQ (Gen 1) — ≈100 tiles
  • Edge 1050 / 1040 / 1030 Plus — ≈250 tiles (4 files max)
  • Edge 1030 (orig.), 840, 830, 540, 530, 810, 800 — ≈100 tiles
  • GPSMAP 62 family, 79 / 86 / 276Cx — ≈100 tiles
  • Oregon 700 / 750, eTrex 20x / 22x / 32x / Touch — ≈100 tiles
  • zumo XT / XT2 / 590 / 595 — ≈100 tiles
✗ Not supported

Firmware doesn't read KMZ at all. There's no workaround.

  • Epix (Gen 1, 2014)
  • Fenix 5 / 5S (no map)
  • Forerunner 935 and earlier
  • Instinct (all, incl. 2 / 2X / Crossover / 3)
  • Venu / Vivoactive (any)
  • Edge 510 / 520 / 520 Plus

Heads-up: the only device the maintainer has personally tested is the Epix 2. Everything else is grouped from Garmin support docs, community forum threads, and the firmware-family relationships between models. Treat the bucketing as a best-effort guess for your specific watch, not a guarantee. Two known quirks behind the grouping: (1) The Fenix 7 / Epix 2 / Fenix 8 generation is shown here as 500-tile based on the maintainer's empirical build (~489 tiles render fine on an Epix 2); Garmin docs and most community sources still quote 100. Pro and non-Pro share the same firmware family, so both sit in the same bucket. If your watch turns out to behave as 100, switch to the custom-area picker. (2) The original Fenix 5X (2017) inherited the 500-tile cap from Garmin's handheld firmware family. Starting with the Fenix 5 Plus (2018) and through the Fenix 6 generation, the cap was reduced to 100 with no published explanation from Garmin.
