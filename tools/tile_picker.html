How this works

Each cell is 10 × 10 km in LV95. At LK25-native 2.5 m/px one cell costs roughly 10 tiles on the watch (15 raw, minus what gets dropped by the empty-tile mask). The 500-cap is about 50 cells of LK25-native coverage.

The output is a selection file you can feed into the local build script to produce a custom KMZ pack matching your selection.

Plain drag still pans the map. While Ctrl or Shift is held, the map cursor changes and the Controls panel above highlights the active mode.

Scattered cells are fine. The build pipeline only emits tiles inside the cells you picked. Garmin happily renders non-contiguous tiles.
