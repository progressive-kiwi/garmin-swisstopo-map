Install swisstopo maps on your Garmin watch

Works on Epix 2 / Epix Pro / Fenix 7 Pro / Fenix 8. There's a note for Fenix 6 at the bottom. No special software needed, we just copy files.

Step 1. Pick which maps you want

  • Region picker, click your region on the map (or search a place) and get direct download links for its pack.
  • Custom builder, paint your own area and have a tailor-made pack built (typically within an hour).

Each regional pack now includes its own <zone>_overview.kmz (zoom-out coverage to about 12 km). The picker bundles it automatically — you don't need a separate all-Switzerland background file any more.

Step 2. Plug the watch into your computer

Use the USB cable that came with the watch. A phone-charging cable will not work, those only carry power, not data. The watch appears as a USB drive named GARMIN.

Step 3. Open the Garmin/CustomMaps/ folder

On the GARMIN drive, go to:

GARMIN/Garmin/CustomMaps/

(Yes, the word Garmin appears twice.) If CustomMaps/ doesn't exist, create it. The name must be exactly CustomMaps, capital C, capital M, no space.

Step 4. Clean out old .kmz files

✅ DELETE: every file inside CustomMaps/ that ends in .kmz. Those are old maps. They take up your tile budget.
❌ DON'T DELETE: any .img file, anywhere on the GARMIN drive. Those are the standard maps that came with your watch (Garmin TopoActive, the basemap). The watch keeps working without them, but you'll lose the default map background, and getting it back means re-downloading it through Garmin Express.

Folders to leave alone, even if they look empty:

  • Garmin/Maps/
  • Garmin/Maps/MapInstall/
  • anything containing .img, .gma, .unl, or .jnx

Rule of thumb: only delete files ending in .kmz, and only inside Garmin/CustomMaps/.

Step 5. Copy the new .kmz files in

Drag every .kmz you downloaded into Garmin/CustomMaps/. A regional pack is a small bundle: a few detail files plus its overview file. They all go in the same folder.

GARMIN/
└── Garmin/
    └── CustomMaps/
        ├── 02_wallis_west_part_001.kmz
        ├── 02_wallis_west_part_002.kmz
        ├── 02_wallis_west_part_003.kmz
        ├── 02_wallis_west_part_004.kmz
        └── 02_wallis_west_overview.kmz
Copy one region at a time. Your watch has a limit on how many map pieces it can show at once. Each regional bundle (detail parts plus its overview file) is sized to fit. If you copy more than one region, the watch will quietly leave some pieces out, and you don't get to choose which.

Step 6. Safely eject

  • Windows: right-click GARMIN in This PC → Eject.
  • Mac: drag the drive to the Trash.
  • Linux: right-click → Eject.

Step 7. Reboot the watch and enable Custom Maps

  1. Hold the Light button (top-left) for ~15 seconds until the watch powers off, then press it again to power back on.
  2. Hold the back button → Map → Map Settings → Map Layers (sometimes labelled "Configure Map" or "Configure Maps").
  3. Make sure Custom Maps is set to On.

Done. Open the map screen, the LK25 raster with slope shading and routes is there.

⚠ On some Epix and Fenix firmware versions the individual KMZ files do show up in Map Manager and can be toggled on/off there. On others, they all render as a single combined Custom Maps layer with one toggle. Either way, the watch caps how many tiles render at once, so swapping regions is still done via USB file moves rather than the Map Manager toggle.

Switching regions later

  1. Plug in, open Garmin/CustomMaps/.
  2. Delete every .kmz from the old region (detail parts and the _overview.kmz).
  3. Copy the new region's .kmz in.
  4. Eject, reboot.

Troubleshooting

Map looks blank. Did you reboot? Did you enable Map Layers → Custom Maps? Are the .kmz files in Garmin/CustomMaps/ and not Garmin/Maps/?

Only part of the region renders. Over the tile limit. Keep only one regional pack at a time (detail parts plus its _overview.kmz).

No standard Garmin map (TopoActive) any more. An .img file was deleted. Plug the watch in and reinstall the basemap via Garmin Express.

Watch hangs on boot. Unplug, hold Light for 30 seconds, retry. If it persists, remove the most recently added .kmz.

Fenix 6 / 6 Pro / 6X Pro

The standard packs don't fit on the Fenix 6 family, those watches cap at 100 tiles total (compared to roughly 500 on Epix 2 / Fenix 7 Pro) and 3 MB per KMZ. A coarser Fenix 6 build is in progress but not yet shipped. Until then, the region picker still works for trip planning; for the on-watch map, you'd fall back to Garmin TOPO Schweiz v2 PRO.
